# ASSCAT Campus AI Copilot - Pitch

## Problem Statement
The old ASSCAT student portal uses a traditional menu-based interface that is often confusing, slow, and difficult to navigate. Students need to click through multiple pages just to complete simple tasks like checking clearance, schedules, or announcements. This leads to wasted time, frustration, and poor user experience.

## Industry 5.0 Justification
The ASSCAT Campus AI Copilot follows Industry 5.0 principles by focusing on human-centered technology. Instead of replacing students, the AI assists them by understanding their needs through natural language (chat or voice). It enhances productivity, reduces effort, and provides personalized responses, making the system more intuitive and efficient.

## Agile Requirements (User Stories)

1. As a student, I want to ask the AI about my clearance status, so that I can quickly know if I am eligible for enrollment.

2. As a student, I want the AI to show my class schedule, so that I don’t need to manually search through multiple menus.

3. As a student, I want to receive announcements through the AI, so that I stay updated in real-time.
